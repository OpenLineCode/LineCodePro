Model endpoints now accept plain http:// addresses on localhost, emulator, private/LAN and link-local hosts, just like MCP servers, and the HTTP allowance in Security settings also admits a public host. A pasted base URL without a scheme or with full-width characters is repaired instead of rejected, and an unusable endpoint now reports the reason and the address in the error log; such configuration failures are no longer retried three times or reported as UNKNOWN.
